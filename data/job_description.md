# Graduate Engineer Programme 2027 — Thara Energy

**Intake:** 80 places · **Start date:** June 2027 · **Location:** Bangkok head office, Rayong refinery & petrochemical complex, Songkhla offshore supply base and offshore platforms in the Gulf of Thailand

## About the programme
Thara Energy is an integrated Thai energy company: we explore for and produce oil and gas offshore, run a refinery and a petrochemical plant, and sell fuel through our trading and retail arm. Many of our most experienced engineers will retire over the next ten years, and the business is shifting toward gas, renewables and low-carbon projects such as carbon capture and storage (CCS) and hydrogen. The Graduate Engineer Programme is how we build the next generation of Offshore Installation Managers, Refinery Operations Managers, Reservoir Engineers and Process Engineers — and the engineers who will lead our energy transition.

Graduates complete a **two-year rotational programme** (three to four rotations of six to eight months) with a mentor and a structured technical curriculum, then move into a permanent engineering role.

## Tracks
| Track | Typical disciplines | Example rotations |
|---|---|---|
| Upstream | Petroleum, Chemical, Mechanical | Reservoir engineering, production engineering, offshore operations (14/14 rotation) |
| Downstream | Chemical, Mechanical, Instrumentation & Control | Refinery process engineering, petrochemical operations, turnaround planning |
| Engineering & Maintenance | Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation & Control | Rotating equipment, electrical & power systems, control systems |
| New Energy & Digital | Chemical, Environmental, Electrical, Computer, Energy | CCS project team, solar & hydrogen ventures, operations data & digital twins |

## What you will do
- Solve real engineering problems in operating plants, offshore facilities and projects.
- Work safely in high-hazard environments, following permit-to-work and process-safety systems.
- Use data and digital tools (for example Python, process simulation, analytics) to improve reliability, efficiency and emissions.
- Contribute to low-carbon projects as Thara Energy decarbonises its portfolio.
- Work in multidisciplinary teams across sites, and present your work to senior leaders.

## Who we are looking for
**Eligibility (must have)**
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Petroleum, Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation & Control, Environmental, Computer, Industrial or Energy Engineering.
- Graduated, or graduating, between 2025 and 2027.
- Willing to work on rotation at operating sites, including offshore platforms.
- Right to work in Thailand.

**What makes a strong candidate**
- A solid technical foundation in your discipline, shown through coursework, a thesis or senior project.
- Hands-on engineering experience: internships, co-operative education, plant or field work, or substantial practical projects.
- A safety mindset: you take hazards seriously, follow procedures and speak up.
- Problem-solving with data: you can analyse a problem, use numbers and tools, and turn findings into action.
- Genuine interest in the energy transition (CCS, hydrogen, renewables, efficiency, emissions reduction).
- Collaboration and leadership: you have worked well in teams and taken responsibility.
- Clear communication in English and Thai.

We welcome applications from all universities and backgrounds. We assess what you have done and how you did it — not where you studied.
